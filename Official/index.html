A word from our mentor

Entrepreneurship drives innovation, creates jobs, and fuels economic growth. At ABES E-Cell, we inspire students to transform ideas into impactful ventures by fostering an entrepreneurial mindset and providing the right guidance, mentorship, and opportunities. Through flagship events like E-Summit, workshops, startup meetups, and The Entrepreneur Show (TES), we empower aspiring entrepreneurs to innovate, collaborate, and build successful startups. Join us and be a part of a thriving community shaping the future of entrepreneurship.

Mahendra Kumar Gupta Mentor, E-CELL, ABES Engineering College, Ghaziabad.
