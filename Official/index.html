§ 02 — About

Not a club. A pipeline.

Most people meet us at an event. What they don't see is the year-round work — the mentor calls, the pitch reviews, the 1am "can you look at this deck" messages — that turns a hallway conversation into a company.

We exist to shorten the distance between having an idea and knowing if it works. Everything we run, from workshops to our flagship summit, is built around that one job.

Ideate

Open sessions, hangouts, and campus talks that turn "what if" into a written-down thesis.

Innovate

Bootcamps and mentor sprints where the thesis gets pressure-tested against real users.

Incubate

Seed connects, internship pipelines, and investor rooms for the ideas that hold up.
